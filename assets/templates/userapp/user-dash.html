About Maternity Assistance

Maternity Assistance is your digital companion through pregnancy and childbirth planning. We combine your health information with trusted clinical models to help you understand possible delivery options, stay informed at every stage, and connect with our care team when you need support.

Everything you see on this dashboard — predictions, messages, and appointments — is private to your account. Use the tools below to explore features at your own pace, and always discuss important health decisions with your doctor or midwife.

What you can do here

  • Receive childbirth mode insights based on your clinical details
  • Get trimester tips and answers from the pregnancy assistant
  • Message our support team privately, with file sharing when needed
  • Book consultations and follow appointment updates in real time
  • Review your full prediction history whenever you need it
